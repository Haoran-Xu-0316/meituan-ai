# SkillPal card photographs

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These are fictional learning scenes, not photographs of the demo members. The current twelve partner cards each use a different photograph. Three original scenes remain in use: coding, design, and music. Other original category scenes are retained as unused assets.

Original PNG files are preserved in result/card-photo-originals locally. Published WebP files retain the original dimensions and use quality 82 encoding; no crop or scene edits were applied.

## coding.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cover photograph for a Chinese skills exchange demo SkillPal. Landscape 3:2 composition. Candid editorial photograph, soft natural window light, real everyday textures, restrained warm neutrals and one small yellow accent. Close practical learning scene, subject mainly central/right, keep lower left relatively simple for a website title overlay. No printed titles, logos, watermarks, collages, glossy stock-photo poses or identifiable faces. Two adult peers learning Python at a modest wooden desk. Over-shoulder detail: one laptop with a softly out-of-focus code editor, notebook with small handwritten flowchart, person's hand on trackpad, a yellow pencil. Authentic lived-in study workspace.
